# Six-week creative technology program

The program runs six weeks, Saturday mornings 10:00–13:00, with a published intake path and an exit ticket.
It is a creative technology lab for artists and cultural staff, not a generic coding bootcamp.

## Scope

- Six Saturday sessions, 10:00–13:00.
- One shared lab, equipment checkout, and a help queue.
- Intake questions collect goals and prior tool use. They do not collect private applicant scoring.

## Out of scope

- Autonomous email to participants.
- Attendance or learning-gain KPIs that have not been measured.
- Fine-tuning a model on student work.
