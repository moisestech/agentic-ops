# Network readiness and approvals

The DCC Index is a structured snapshot of partner and constituent readiness:
consent, digital orientation, relationship stage, and missing fields.

After network signup, proposed actions are written as **drafts** — invite copy,
follow-up tasks, or missing-info asks. A human must approve the draft before
anything is treated as executed.

There is no auto-send. Approval status moves from pending to approved, rejected,
or needs_edit. Only an approved draft may be filed as an internal execution
record in this reference implementation.
