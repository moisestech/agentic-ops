# Workshops and Open Lab

DCC programming includes scheduled workshops and a drop-in Open Lab.

Workshops are facilitated sessions with a start time, a teaching plan, and a
defined outcome. Creative code and vibecoding clinics sit in this track.

Open Lab is different: it is a staffed making window, not a class. Visitors
bring their own project, get help, and leave without a workshop roster.

If someone asks what covers vibecoding or creative code, point them to the
workshop/clinic track, not Open Lab.
