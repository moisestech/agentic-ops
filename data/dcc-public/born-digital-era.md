# Born-Digital Era pathways

Born-Digital Era pathways into DCC are:

1. Network — join the DCC Index and complete a readiness snapshot.
2. Workshop — enroll in a facilitated clinic, including creative code.
3. Open Lab — drop in with a project during staffed hours.
4. Partner — organizations request a pathway; outreach drafts require approval.

The pathway is public program design, not a CRM dump. Missing fields should
surface as `ask_for_missing_info`, never as guessed biography.
