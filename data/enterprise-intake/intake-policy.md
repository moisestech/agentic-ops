# Intake ticket policy

Every intake ticket must cite an approved policy passage before a first decision is shown.
Unsupported evidence ids fail closed and route to human review.

## Required fields

- Ticket summary
- Requested system
- Whether personal data is present
- Whether a write to a system of record is requested

## Routing

High-confidence, cited, read-only answers may be shown to the requester.
Any write, any missing citation, and any PII-bearing ticket pauses for a reviewer.
